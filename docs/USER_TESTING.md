# Testing UNIVERSE.EXE with five first-time users

Goal: find out whether a stranger understands the app and reaches a "wow" moment quickly. Do not explain anything first. Watch, do not help, take notes.

**Setup:** a fresh browser profile (so the welcome card shows), the deployed URL, desktop Chrome or Edge. Screen recording on if the person agrees. Tell them only: "Please think aloud. Try to do what feels interesting."

## Tasks and what to record
| # | Task (say it) | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Open the page and show me what you think this is." | Seconds until they click anything; first thing they click; what they say it is |
| 2 | (Welcome card visible) Do not prompt. | Did they press Break Reality? Seconds from page load to gravity visibly reversing (**target: under 30**) |
| 3 | "What just happened?" | Can they say gravity reversed because of a rule? |
| 4 | "Go back and try a challenge." | Found Challenges? Solved Gravity Escape? Minutes taken; where stuck; used the hint? |
| 5 | "Make something happen when two spheres collide." | Found Reality recipes? Added Chain Reaction? Saw the new spheres? |
| 6 | "Send a friend your experiment." | Found Share? Link copied? |
| 7 | Phone, if available: open the same page. | Any overlap, unreachable button, or blank 3D area? |

## After the session (ask)
1. In one sentence, what is this? 2. What was confusing? 3. Which moment felt best? 4. Would you send it to someone? (yes/no, why)

## Scoring sheet (one row per person)
Time to first Break Reality: ___ s | Understood "rule changes physics": Y/N | Solved challenge 1: Y/N | Used hint: Y/N | Console errors seen (F12): Y/N | Biggest confusion: ___ | Wow moment: ___

**Pass bar:** at least 4 of 5 reach Break Reality in under 30 s and can say what it did. Anything that two or more people trip on is the next fix. Report results to the team; do not claim "user-tested" until this is done.
